How to select your
website direction.

Open the live demos and focus on how they feel, what information they show and how quickly a customer can take action. Every colour, photo, word and feature can be changed later.

01

Check the first screen

Does the opening section clearly tell customers what the business offers and what they should do next?

02

Test on mobile

Open every shortlisted demo on your phone. Check menu, buttons, forms and the complete scrolling experience.

03

Follow the customer journey

Try to book, call, WhatsApp, order or enquire. The most important customer action should feel effortless.

04

Choose useful sections

You may select one complete demo or combine the hero, gallery, services and contact flow from different demos.

05

List business content

Prepare your logo, services, products, photos, pricing, team, location, contact details and customer reviews.

06

Mark required features

Decide whether you need booking, payment, admin panel, WhatsApp, maps, gallery, blog, login or order management.

Remember

The demo is a starting direction, not the final website. AJAY NXT will adapt the selected experience around the actual business, brand language and customer goals.
